Every card swipe on a PayDuka PoS triggers instant on-chain settlement — merchants get paid immediately, fees are reimbursed, and PDuka supply deflates with every transaction.

01

💳 Customer Taps or Swipes

The merchant's PayDuka terminal accepts the payment. The PoS broadcasts a signed transaction to Polygon in under 2 seconds.

sub-2s response

How Cash-In Works

01

💵 Walk Into Any PayDuka Merchant

The customer visits any spaza shop, convenience store, or retailer in the PayDuka network. No appointment, no forms, no bank account needed — just cash and a phone number or PayDuka wallet ID.

zero paperwork
02

📱 Merchant Enters Deposit on PoS

The merchant opens the PayDuka app, selects "Cash-In", enters the customer's wallet ID or phone number, and keys in the deposit amount. Customer hands over cash. Merchant confirms.

merchant verified
03

⚡ Instant On-Chain Credit

PayDuka's smart contract mints the equivalent ZARP (ZAR-pegged stablecoin) directly into the customer's wallet on Polygon. The customer sees the balance update in under 2 seconds. Both parties receive an SMS and in-app confirmation.

sub-2s settlement
04

💰 Merchant Earns Commission

The merchant earns a flat commission on every cash-in transaction — an additional revenue stream on top of normal sales. Commissions are credited in ZARP and can be withdrawn or spent immediately. The more deposits processed, the more the merchant earns.

new revenue stream

How Cash Remittance Works

R1

🔄 Sender Deposits Cash at Merchant A

A worker in Johannesburg walks into a PayDuka merchant, hands over R500 in cash, and provides the recipient's phone number in another city — or even another country. The sender pays a flat fee of R5–R10 (vs R37–R74 at traditional remittance services charging 7.4%).

flat fee · up to 90% cheaper
R2

⚡ Instant Transfer via Blockchain

The ZARP equivalent is transferred on Polygon in under 2 seconds — no SWIFT, no correspondent banks, no 3-day wait. The recipient receives an SMS notification immediately with a unique collection code.

sub-2s · cross-border capable
R3

💵 Recipient Collects Cash at Merchant B

The recipient walks into any PayDuka merchant in their area — a spaza shop in Limpopo, a tuck shop in Nairobi, a convenience store in Maputo. They present their code or phone number, and the merchant dispenses cash. Alternatively, the recipient can keep the funds in their PayDuka wallet and spend digitally.

collect anywhere · or keep digital
MERCHANT BENEFIT

New Revenue Without New Inventory

Every cash-in or cash-out transaction earns the merchant a commission — turning existing foot traffic into financial-services revenue. M-Pesa agents earn up to 40% of their income from commissions alone. PayDuka brings the same model on-chain.

CUSTOMER BENEFIT

Financial Access Without a Bank

No bank account required. No minimum balance. No monthly fees. Customers load their PayDuka wallet with cash at any merchant and use it to pay bills, buy airtime, send money, or shop at any PayDuka-accepting store — all from their phone.

NETWORK EFFECT

Every Merchant Is a Branch

Standard Bank expanded Instant Money to 400,000+ cashout points at spaza shops — proving the model. PayDuka goes further: merchants are both payment acceptance points AND cash-in/cash-out agents, creating a dual-revenue loop that accelerates adoption.

🌍 The Cash-In Flywheel

More merchants → more cash-in points → more wallets funded → more digital payments at merchants → more merchant revenue → more merchants join. The cash-in network is the growth engine that makes the entire PayDuka ecosystem self-reinforcing.

Every payment AND every cash-in/cash-out transaction processed through the PayDuka network triggers the same automated on-chain split — increasing treasury, burning supply, and settling value instantly.
